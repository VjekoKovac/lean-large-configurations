# Reused Lean proofs

The table identifies Mathlib declarations used in the number-theoretic construction. The project imports them from Mathlib v4.35.0. The links point to the commit pinned by `lake-manifest.json`. Mathlib records its authors and Apache 2.0 license in the linked source files. [Pinned Mathlib tree](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679)

| Step in the paper | Existing proof or definition | Use in this project |
|---|---|---|
| Choosing the prime denominator | `Nat.exists_prime_lt_and_le_two_mul`, formalized by Patrick Stevens and Bolton Bailey. [Bertrand](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/NumberTheory/Bertrand.lean) | `exists_prime_between` excludes the composite upper endpoint for inputs at least two. `weyl_parameters` applies the resulting lemma to a power of the pattern size. |
| Averaging shifted exponential sums | `sq_sum_le_card_mul_sum_sq`, in the module by Mantas Bakšys and Yaël Dillies. [Chebyshev inequalities](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Algebra/Order/Chebyshev.lean) | `van_der_corput` applies the finite Cauchy–Schwarz estimate to the sums over shifts. Its translation and endpoint error estimates are proved in the project. |
| Degree of the differenced phase | `Polynomial.taylor_eval`, `Polynomial.taylor_coeff`, `Polynomial.hasseDeriv_coeff`, and `Polynomial.natDegree_hasseDeriv_le`, in modules by Johan Commelin. [Taylor expansion](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/Taylor.lean) · [Hasse derivative](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/HasseDeriv.lean) | `shiftedDifference_degree` and `shiftedDifference_coeff` compute the cancellation of the leading term and the coefficient of the next degree for two different shifts. |
| Fourier phase and complete linear sums | `Real.fourierChar`, `Complex.isPrimitiveRoot_exp_of_isCoprime`, and `IsPrimitiveRoot.geom_sum_eq_zero`. [Circle character](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Analysis/Complex/Circle.lean) · [Complex roots](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/RingTheory/RootsOfUnity/Complex.lean) · [Primitive roots](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/RingTheory/RootsOfUnity/PrimitiveRoots.lean) | `linear_phase_sum` removes the constant phase, then uses the vanishing geometric sum for the prime denominator. |
| Short-arc weight outside its supporting interval | `Real.mul_abs_le_abs_sin`, the lower bound for sine on the centered half-period. [Trigonometric bounds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Bounds.lean) | `fourier_chord_lower` turns the sine estimate into the circle chord bound in terms of distance to an integer. `fejer_sum_tail` applies this to the finite geometric sum. |
| Sampling without replacement | `Finset.card_powersetCard`, `Nat.choose_succ_right_eq`, and `Finset.card_biUnion_le`. [Finite subsets](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Data/Finset/Powerset.lean) · [Binomial coefficients](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Data/Nat/Choose/Basic.lean) · [Finite union bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Algebra/BigOperators/Group/Finset/Basic.lean) | `choose_le_fraction_pow` bounds the ratio of binomial coefficients. `exists_hitting_of_sum_choose_lt` counts the bad samples. `exists_hitting_of_exp_lt` obtains a single sample meeting every grid target. |

The original mathematical argument is due to Vjekoslav Kovač and Adian Anibal Santos Sepčić. Section 3 of their paper credits ChatGPT 5.4 Pro for suggesting the random thinning step. The new Lean proofs of the specialized Fourier estimates, explicit numerical inequalities, and thinning argument were generated with AI assistance. [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18544v1) · [Proof and parameter choices](fourier-proof.md)

## Geometry and Lebesgue measure

The following declarations identify the Mathlib results used in the other route through the proof. These are imported lemmas in the pinned distribution. The proofs specific to the paper, including the classification of the finite cross, are in this project.

| Mathematical step | Mathlib declarations used | Project proof |

| Equality in the triangle inequality and the affine label of each point | `sameRay_iff_norm_sub`, `SameRay.norm_smul_eq`, `Finset.min'_lt_max'_of_card` | [Collinear.lean](../Density/Collinear.lean) |
